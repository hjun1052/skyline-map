No .cslmap file yet? Here's how to get one

Need your file?

Skyline Map reads the .cslmap file the CSLMapView mod writes from your city.

1

Export it from the game

  1. Subscribe to CSLMapView on the Steam Workshop and enable it in Content Manager.
  2. Load your city, open the mod's options and press Export.
  3. Optional: turn on exporting every time you save, so the newest file is always there.
Read the full guide
2

Find it on your computer

CSLMapView saves your exports in the CSLMapView folder of your Cities: Skylines install:

  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Steam/steamapps/common/Cities_Skylines/CSLMapView
  • Windows: C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Cities_Skylines\CSLMapView
  • Linux: ~/.local/share/Steam/steamapps/common/Cities_Skylines/CSLMapView
