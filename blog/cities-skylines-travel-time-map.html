Blog / Travel-time maps

How far can you get in 30 minutes? Travel-time maps for your Cities: Skylines city

A travel-time map answers one question at a glance: from here, where can I get to in half an hour? Skyline Map draws one for any spot in your city, using your real roads and transit lines.

November 9, 2026

Planners call these isochrone maps. They are one of the quickest ways to see whether a neighbourhood is well connected, and in Cities: Skylines they show which areas your metro actually serves and which ones only look close on the map.

Make one

  1. Open your export at skyline.codz.me (see the walkthrough if you haven't yet).
  2. Right-click the spot you care about and choose How far can I get from here? On a phone, use the menu: it starts from the middle of the screen.
  3. Pick Driving, Transit, Walking or Cycling, and a limit of 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes.

The map shades everything you can reach in four bands, darkest for the nearest quarter of the time, and the panel shows the reachable land area. Click anywhere else, or drag the pin, to try another start.

How the times are worked out

The times come from the same engine as Directions, so the two always agree:

  • Driving follows the roads cars may use, obeys one-way streets and adds time at junctions and turns. A place only counts as reached by car near an ordinary road, because you can't stop on a motorway or a ramp.
  • Transit walks to a stop, waits the estimated average time for that mode, rides at speeds taken from the track or road, and can change lines. Waits are estimates, since the export has no timetables.
  • Walking and cycling use pavements, paths and bike lanes.

From the network, the last stretch to a place is on foot, up to 300 m. Water is never shaded.

What it's good for

Finding transit gaps

Start at a busy station and pick Transit. Shading that follows the lines in thin ribbons means people only benefit right next to the stops. A district that stays unshaded at 30 minutes, while it is close by car, is a good candidate for your next line or a feeder bus.

Comparing modes

Switch between Driving and Transit from the same start. When the transit area is a small fraction of the car area, your citizens have a good reason to drive.

Checking a new connection

Build a new line or bridge in game, export again and compare the same start before and after. The reachable area is a simple number to track.
