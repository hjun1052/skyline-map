Toll roads and transit fares for your Cities: Skylines city

Toll booths and ticket prices change how a city feels to get around. Skyline Map finds the toll booths in your export and lets you set the prices yourself, so routes show what a trip would cost.

November 9, 2026

Toll booths on the map

Toll booths in your export get a marker on the map. Click one to see which road it sits on and which roads it connects.

Which routes pay a toll

A driving route is marked "This route has tolls" only when it actually drives through a booth's gate, not just because it uses a highway somewhere near one. The steps include a toll step where the gate is passed.

Turn on Avoid tolls in the driving options to route around the gates. If there is no way around, you still get the tolled route.

Setting the prices

CSLMapView doesn't export toll fees or ticket prices, so you set them yourself. Open the menu and choose Tolls, fares & currency.

Toll fees

Set one default fee for every booth in the city, then change it for any booth that should cost more or less. Driving routes then show the toll total, and each toll step shows its fee.

Transit fares

Set fares per mode (bus, metro, train and so on) or per line. Each fare is a base price plus optional distance surcharges, charged on the distance you actually ride.

Then choose how transfers work:

  • Pay every ride: each boarding is a new fare.
  • One ticket per journey: one fare covers transfers.
  • Distance-based: the whole journey is priced on total distance, like Seoul's integrated fares.

You can limit transfers with a time window and a maximum number of transfers.

Currency

Pick a currency or set your own symbol, where it goes and how many decimals to show. Every price in the app follows it.

Where your prices are kept

Prices are saved in your browser for each city, not in the map file. When you open a newer export of the same city, they are matched back to the same booths and lines by id, name and position, so they carry over. Prices that no longer match anything are kept, not thrown away.

You can export your prices as a JSON file and import them on another computer or browser.

Where prices show up

  • Driving directions: the toll total on each route, and the fee at each toll step.
  • Transit directions: the fare on each option, and a fare breakdown in the itinerary.
  • Line panels: the fare for that line.

More on how routes are planned in directions in your city.
