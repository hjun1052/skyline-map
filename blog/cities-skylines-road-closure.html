What happens if a bridge closes? Test your Cities: Skylines network

Some roads hold a whole city together and others barely matter. Close any piece of your network in Skyline Map and see what changes: the detour, the lines cut, the places cut off.

November 9, 2026

Close something

  1. Open your export at skyline.codz.me.
  2. Choose What if it closes? in the menu, or press the button of the same name on any road, railway or station's info panel.
  3. Tap a road, bridge, tunnel, railway or station on the map.

A tap on an ordinary road closes the stretch between the junctions at either end. A bridge or tunnel closes as a whole, with the other carriageway of a divided road. For a named road you can also close all of it at once.

What you see

The verdict

One line sums it up: No way around, Long detour, Noticeable detour, Barely matters or No trips affected. It comes straight from the trip times below it, and it says when only one way of travelling is stuck, for example a bridge cars can drive round but people on foot can't.

Trips across it

Each direction a car may legally drive the closed stretch gets a row, plus a row on foot, with bars for the time before and after. Tap a row to see the old route and the detour on the map.

Lines and stations

Transit lines that run over the closure are cut there. The parts on either side keep running, and the trips between the stations either side of the cut are timed again. Close a station and its lines run through without stopping; the panel shows the nearest station that is still open and how far it is to walk.

What can you reach in 30 minutes?

A pin starts on one side of the closure (or at the station). Choose driving, transit, walking or cycling and the map shades everywhere within 30 minutes: before, after, or the change, from no change to more than 15 minutes slower, or lost altogether. Drag the pin to start somewhere else.

What it does and doesn't model

  • Routing: the same routing as Directions, with the same one-way rules, turn costs, walking paths and transit waits.
  • Cut off: roads that can no longer be reached from the rest of the network, or left again, by car or on foot.
  • No diversions: lines over the closure are cut, not rerouted, because there's no way to know how you'd reroute them.
  • No traffic: the export has no traffic, passenger or cost figures, so nothing here estimates them. It shows how the network connects, not how busy it would get.

Ideas

  • Find your weak links. Close each bridge to an island or across a river. A "No way around" is a place that needs a second crossing.
  • Check a new line. Close an old station and see whether the new line gives people somewhere close to go instead.
  • Plan roadworks. Before demolishing a road in game, close it here and look at the detour first.
