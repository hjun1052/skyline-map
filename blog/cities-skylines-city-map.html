How to turn your Cities: Skylines city into an interactive map

Cities: Skylines shows your city one camera angle at a time. With the CSLMapView mod and a browser you can see all of it as a proper map: every road, building and transit line, with directions and a transit diagram on top.

November 9, 2026

This guide takes a city from your save game to a map you can pan, search and plan trips on. It takes about five minutes the first time, and seconds after that.

What you need

  • Cities: Skylines (the original game, not Cities: Skylines II).
  • The CSLMapView mod by gansaku, from the Steam Workshop. It writes your city to a .cslmap file.
  • A browser with WebGL, which is any current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

The mod ships with its own viewer, CSLMapView.exe, which draws a map image from the file. Its author lists it as 64-bit Windows only. Skyline Map reads the same file in a browser, so it also works on a Mac or Linux machine.

Step 1: export the city in game

  1. Subscribe to CSLMapView on the Workshop and enable it in Content Manager.
  2. Load your city.
  3. Open the mod's options and press Export. The mod also has an Export and launch the viewer button, which opens CSLMapView.exe afterwards on Windows. You don't need it for this.

The mod can also export every time you save the game, if you turn that on in its settings. Then your newest file is always there when you want it.

Step 2: find the file

Exports go into a folder called CSLMapView inside the game's install folder. On a default Steam install that is:

  • Windows: C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Cities_Skylines\CSLMapView
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Steam/steamapps/common/Cities_Skylines/CSLMapView
  • Linux: ~/.local/share/Steam/steamapps/common/Cities_Skylines/CSLMapView

The file is named after your city, for example Sarrant-20261107-234707.cslmap when timestamps are on, or .cslmap.gz when compression is on. If your Steam library lives somewhere else, see where CSLMapView saves your files for a quick way to get there.

Step 3: open it in Skyline Map

Go to skyline.codz.me and drop the file on the page, or click the drop zone and pick it. The start screen also shows the folder for your system with a button to copy the path, and the shortcut that jumps straight to it in the file picker.

The file is read inside your browser. It isn't uploaded anywhere, so it works the same for a city you'd rather keep to yourself.

Large cities take a few seconds to build: the map draws roads, lots, buildings, water, forests and terrain from the raw data, then the transit network on top.

What you can do with it

Read it like a real map

Roads are drawn at their true width, with highways, arterials, local streets and paths each in their own style. Zoned lots are tinted by use, with buildings inside them, and every street name is placed along the whole street rather than repeated on each piece. Station and building labels use the names you gave them in game.

Tilt it, or switch styles

Right-drag to rotate and tilt. Buildings rise in 3D when the camera tilts, and the game's heightmap gives you hillshading, optional 3D terrain and a Terrain style with contour lines. There is also a Night style.

Plan trips

Directions work for driving, transit, walking and cycling, on your city's own road network and transit lines. How that works is in route planning in your city.

See the transit network as a diagram

The Transit map button draws a metro-style diagram of your lines automatically. More in making a transit map of your city.

See how far you can get

Right-click any spot and the map shades everywhere you can reach in 15 to 60 minutes. See travel-time maps.

Watch it grow

Load older exports and drag a slider through your city's history. See making a time-lapse.

Measure, search and share

The measure tool gives distance, area and an elevation profile. Search finds stations, roads and landmarks. You can also save the current view as a PNG.

Common questions

Does it work with Cities: Skylines II?

No. CSLMapView is a mod for the first game, and Skyline Map reads its export format.

Do I need the mod's Windows viewer?

No. Skyline Map only needs the .cslmap file, so the viewer doesn't matter on a Mac or Linux machine.

The map looks out of date

An export is a snapshot. Export again after you build, or turn on export on save, and open the newest file.
