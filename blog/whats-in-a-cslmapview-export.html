What a CSLMapView export contains, and what it doesn't

A .cslmap file is a snapshot of your city written out by the CSLMapView mod. Knowing what is in it explains what a map made from it can show, and where it has to estimate.

November 9, 2026

A .cslmap file is XML, and .cslmap.gz is the same file compressed. Cities are big, so files of 10–20 MB uncompressed are normal for a developed map.

What's in it

The road and path network

Every network segment is there: roads, highways, paths, rail and metro track, bridges and tunnels. Each one has its shape, its width, its network type, and the type's lanes with their direction, vehicle type and speed limit. Road names are included, plus any custom name you gave.

Nodes (the points segments meet at) have positions and heights. They also carry an "on ground" flag, which tells a real bridge or tunnel apart from a road that only looks raised because the terrain under it is coarse.

Buildings

Every building has its position, footprint, type, and the name you gave it in game, if any. Larger assets made of several buildings, like a station with separate halls and platforms, a stadium's stands or a mall's wings, are linked together in a chain. Skyline Map follows that chain, so clicking any part selects the whole asset.

Transit

Each transit line has its name, type, colour and stops in order. Stations take the names you gave them.

Terrain, water and forests

The heightmap, the water surface and sea level, and a forest density grid. Hillshading, contours, 3D terrain, shorelines and woods all come from these.

Districts and parks

District and park names, each with one label point.

What isn't in it

MissingWhat Skyline Map does about it
District shapesEstimates each district's area from its label point and the transit stops that record their district. A selected district is drawn as a soft region that fades toward its neighbours, and its area is labelled as an estimate.
Reliable one-way directionsWorks them out from junctions, where your transit vehicles actually drive, and keep-right on divided roads. More on this.
Traffic lights, crossings, turn bans, TM:PE rulesNot used. Routes charge time for turns and crossroads in general instead.
TimetablesWaits are estimated and labelled as estimates.
Toll fees and ticket pricesYou set them. See tolls and fares.
Building heightsEstimated from each building's zone and level for the 3D view.

Why the names you give matter

The export includes the names you typed in game for stations and buildings. Those are what appear on the map, in search, on the transit diagram and in transit directions. A station you never named appears as an icon without a label, so naming the important ones makes every view clearer.

Export settings worth knowing

  • Compression writes .cslmap.gz, which is much smaller and opens the same way.
  • Timestamps in file names keep every export instead of overwriting the last one.
  • Export on save writes a new file each time you save the game.
  • Output folder moves exports somewhere other than the default. See where the files go.

Is my city uploaded when I open it?

No. Skyline Map reads the file in your browser and builds the map on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.
