Make a metro-style transit map of your Cities: Skylines city

Real cities explain their transit with a diagram, not a street map. Skyline Map draws one for your Cities: Skylines network automatically, with your line names, colours and stations.

November 9, 2026

Drawing a transit diagram by hand takes hours, and it is out of date the next time you add a line. If you have a CSLMapView export, you can get one in a few seconds and come back for a new one whenever the network changes.

How to make one

  1. Export your city with the CSLMapView mod (see the full walkthrough).
  2. Open the file at skyline.codz.me.
  3. Click the Transit map chip at the top, or choose Transit map from the menu.

The diagram opens over the map. Drag to pan and scroll or pinch to zoom.

What it keeps from your city

The layout is generated, but the content isn't made up. It comes from the network in your export:

  • Station order on every line, as the vehicles run it.
  • Line names and colours as you set them in game.
  • Interchanges, where lines share a station.
  • Shared track, drawn as parallel lines side by side instead of one line on top of another.
  • Branches and loops.
  • Station names you gave in game.

Then the lines are straightened onto horizontal, vertical and 45° runs with even spacing between stations, the way the London and Seoul maps are drawn.

Stations that are one place on the ground

In the game, a metro station and the train station above it are separate buildings with separate stops. A good diagram shows them as one interchange. Skyline Map treats stops within 120 m of each other as one place, or within 260 m when their names match, like "Norisa Station" and "Norisa Ferry Pier". It draws them side by side with a walking-transfer link.

Using it

Follow a line

Tap a line to highlight it and see its stops in order.

Plan from a station

Tap a station to see which lines call there, then use From here or To here to plan a journey without leaving the diagram.

See a route on the diagram

A transit route planned in Directions can be shown on the diagram as well, which makes transfers easy to follow.

Filter by mode

Turn modes on and off: train, metro, monorail, tram, ferry, cable car and bus. Buses start switched off, because a city with hundreds of bus lines reads better without them, but you can turn them on. The diagram remembers your choice.

Tips for a cleaner diagram

  • Name your stations. A station you didn't name shows no label, so a named network reads much better.
  • Give lines distinct colours. The diagram uses the colours from your game.
  • Export again after changes. The diagram is rebuilt from each file, so it never goes stale.

Prefer the real geography? The normal map draws every line in its own colour too, with parallel lines offset so they don't hide each other, and a list of all lines with their stops.
