Your city, mapped for real.

Skyline Map turns Cities: Skylines exports into detailed, interactive maps with real directions and transit planning.

Open your export Take the tour

Free, in your browser. Your file never leaves your computer.

Drawn like a real atlas.

Roads at their true width, every lot and building, rivers, forests and hillshade, all traced from your export.

Tilt it. Walk the skyline.

Buildings rise in 3D when the camera tilts, with heights estimated from each zone and level.

The ground under it.

Elevation, contours and true relief come from the game's own heightmap.

After dark.

A Night style for late planning sessions, down to the transit lines and the 3D buildings.

Directions that know the roads.

Routes run on your city's real network, with alternatives and turn-by-turn steps.

Tolls, found and priced.

Booths are detected from the road network. Every route says which gates it passes and what they cost, and you can avoid them.

How far can you get?

Pick a spot and a way to travel. The map shades everywhere you can reach in 15 to 60 minutes, timed like Directions.

What if it closes?

Close a bridge, a tunnel, a stretch of railway or a station. Trips across it are routed again, before against after.

Every line, timed from the tracks.

Ride times come from real speed limits, stops and dwell times. Fares follow the rules you set.

A transit map, drawn for you.

Your network untangled onto horizontal, vertical and 45° runs, in the style of a metro map, with every station in order.

Your prices, your currency.

Add what the export leaves out: toll fees, fares with distance surcharges and transfer rules. They stay in your browser and carry over to newer exports.

Click anything.

Stations, lines, buildings, districts and booths each open with everything the export knows.

Watch it grow.

Load older exports of your city and drag through time. Roads, buildings and lines appear when they were first built.

Records of your city.

The longest bridge, the deepest station, the loneliest building. Each one is measured from your export and shown on the map.

In your pocket.

Bottom sheets, a directions card and round controls, laid out for phones.

English and 한국어.

Switch at any time. Turn-by-turn directions are written in your language, with Korean particles fitted to every road name.

Shown with the city's original Korean names.
